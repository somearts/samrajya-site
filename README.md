# Samraj

Samraj is a playable, two-player civilisation game in the browser. Create a room or join one with its code, choose a civilisation, and take a place on a looping world board. The live game is ready to play; the source repository also contains the latest refinements under active development.

## A turn changes the map

Roll the die to move around the board, then decide what to do with the place where you land. Claim an unowned property or pass, pay rent when another player owns it, build up a complete set, or sell holdings when your purse needs room. Crossing Start brings a reward, but a useful turn is rarely just about moving forward.

## The board has its own interruptions

Ragnarok and Nexus bring unexpected cards; Epoch bends the next movement or roll. A visit to Fight can put weapons and protected properties into play, while the Black Hole asks a trapped player to choose how to get back out. Each encounter pauses the ordinary route and gives both players a new consequence to consider.

The four civilisations do not all begin in the same way. Their opening advantages, the order of the board, property sets and the changing state of a match make a familiar roll land differently from game to game. Build carefully, keep an eye on the other seat, and leave a little room for the universe to interfere.
